## 1. The marketplace and eligibility

Mlezi is a project operated by Lintech Telecom, registered as a sole proprietorship. The service is a marketplace for adult nannies and babysitters and employers/parents. Its current service provides listings, search, shortlists, reviews and introductions. Families hire and pay nannies and babysitters directly. Mlezi does not currently recruit or place workers, employ/supervise nannies and babysitters, provide immigration services, or become a party to a family’s care/employment agreement.

You must be at least 18, provide accurate information and control the account you use. Do not impersonate someone or share login codes. Keep profile/contact information current. Verification does not authorize you to act for another person.


## 2. Agree the work separately

Families and nannies and babysitters must discuss duties, references, experience, pay, hours, rest, accommodation, transport and safety before agreeing to work together. A nanny profile does not automatically include general cleaning, laundry or whole-household cooking. Travel care needs an agreement about destinations, costs, accommodation and working hours.

Both parties must meet applicable employment, wage, safeguarding, work-authorization and travel requirements. A listing or identity badge grants no permission to work in another country. Do not exploit workers, withhold identity documents or impose unlawful arrangements. These terms do not replace employment rights or determine the status of the actual working relationship.


## 3. Jobs, proposals and visibility

Employers may post jobs and review proposals. A nanny or babysitter proposal includes a message, pay amount and pay period. An employer can offer the job, and only the applicant can accept. A pending-review nanny or babysitter may apply, but administrator approval of adult identity is required before acceptance confirms a hire. Confirmation also requires an active employer contact-access pass or an existing unlock for that nanny or babysitter.

An accepted offer records both parties’ choice on Mlezi and shares their contact information. Agree the complete care and employment terms directly; Mlezi does not process wages or provide escrow. Do not put children’s names, exact home addresses, identity documents or other sensitive information in a job or proposal.

Nannies and babysitters independently choose marketplace visibility and availability. A hire never automatically hides a profile. A hidden profile is still shared with employers to whose jobs the nanny or babysitter applies, and hiding does not revoke previously unlocked contacts. Jobs and proposals are included in the relevant account export and deletion process; deleting an account does not terminate an employment agreement.


## 4. Identity, reviews and Premium

Nannies and babysitters require a profile photo, private ID and recent selfie for manual identity/18+ review. Complete available profiles may be listed for signed-in employers while review is pending, with a clear pending label and no confirmed-age claim. Contact unlocking requires administrator approval. Identity checked is not an issuer-validated guarantee, background/criminal-record check or assurance of suitability. Interview and check references independently. Identity changes or safety concerns can require re-review or restrict a profile.

Reviews must honestly describe an employer’s own experience. Contact unlocking permits review submission but does not prove employment. Do not buy reviews, manipulate ratings, publish private details or post abusive allegations. Nannies and babysitters can reply; users can report concerns and administrators can assess visibility.

Premium is optional paid priority among matching profiles, with rotating exposure. It does not buy an identity badge, better ratings, employment or guaranteed contacts. Search is not a hiring decision by Mlezi.


## 5. Platform plans and payment availability

Browsing, shortlisting and a basic nanny or babysitter profile are free. Employer contact access costs KES 300 for 30 days or KES 500 for 90 days; optional nanny or babysitter Premium costs KES 500 for 365 days. Pay using M-Pesa STK Push in Membership. The amount and duration are displayed before you authorize payment. Platform charges are separate from nanny or babysitter wages.

Renewal is manual. No automatic recurring charge is authorized here. Early renewal extends the existing unexpired term; otherwise a term starts on confirmed activation. Viewing contacts and unlocking new contacts require an active pass. Deletion and identity/safety restrictions can still affect access.

Do not send money manually or follow payment instructions outside the available Membership checkout. Manual Paybill receipt submissions and Paystack checkout are unavailable. M-Pesa STK Push checkout sends a one-off payment request to your phone. Confirm the amount and business on your phone; never enter your M-Pesa PIN on Mlezi. Membership activates after server confirmation, not merely after requesting a prompt. Sandbox confirmations are tests and do not activate membership. For incorrect, duplicate, failed or unrecognized payments, contact Support for investigation and any applicable correction or refund; do not pay again while a report is pending. Rejection of a report does not reverse or refund an M-Pesa transfer automatically. Confirmed platform membership payments are non-refundable for a change of mind, unused access, account deletion or dissatisfaction with a nanny or babysitter. This policy does not exclude refunds, payment corrections or other remedies required by applicable law. Mlezi does not collect nanny or babysitter wages.


## 6. Content and conduct

Only upload information/images you are authorized to share. You retain rights in your content and permit Mlezi to store, display and process it as needed for the requested marketplace services, moderation and the Privacy notice. This does not transfer ownership or authorize unrelated advertising use of identity evidence.

Do not harass, discriminate unlawfully, impersonate, scam, publish children’s or others’ private information, post unlawful content, scrape/resell protected contacts, submit false evidence or attack the service. Never put passwords, OTPs, ID images or payment PINs in public text or support tickets.


## 7. Restrictions and leaving

We may remove content, pause publication, restrict contacts or suspend an account for suspected breaches, identity problems, safety or legal obligations. We explain the basis and offer a review route through Support where appropriate, without compromising privacy or investigations. Platform action does not change legal employment obligations.

You can stop using the service and request deletion through Privacy and account. The Privacy notice explains the two-hour cancellation period, file removal and justified retention. Previously shared information cannot always be recalled. Use Support to request review of cancellation/refund concerns. Any refund must be investigated and processed separately; withdrawing access or rejecting a report does not itself reverse a payment.


## 8. Marketplace role and nanny or babysitter misconduct

Mlezi is a marketplace, not a recruitment agency or the employer or supervisor of nannies and babysitters. Families select, hire, supervise and pay nannies and babysitters directly.

To the extent permitted by applicable law, Mlezi is not responsible for a nanny or babysitter’s independent acts, omissions or misconduct, or disputes arising from the family’s employment or care arrangement. Identity checking and Premium membership do not guarantee conduct, suitability or safety.

We remain responsible for our own platform obligations. Report interruptions, inaccurate information and misconduct for assessment. Nothing here excludes liability or removes consumer, employment, data-protection or other rights that applicable law does not allow us to exclude.


## 9. Support and disputes

Use Support for account, technical, identity, privacy and safety concerns. Signed-out users can submit an issue with their email once delivery/spam protection is configured. Acknowledgements and staff replies are emailed. Our support commitment is to address an issue within 48 hours; it is not emergency response.

These terms are prepared for a Kenya-focused marketplace under applicable Kenyan law. Cross-border arrangements may also involve other local requirements. Support can help seek a practical resolution without waiving access to competent courts, regulators or other lawful remedies. This draft imposes no compulsory private arbitration or blanket release of claims.


## 10. Acceptance and updates

Read the terms and Privacy notice before completing a new account. We record the version accepted at account creation. Imported accounts are not treated as having accepted a version merely because records were migrated. Material changes will be explained and renewed acceptance requested where appropriate before they affect existing users.

Future worker recruitment/placement, managed employment or payment services require updated terms and operational review before introduction.
